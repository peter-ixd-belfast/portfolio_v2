About Me

My passion is to solve problems with innovative design solutions. I enjoy using design to communicate and enagage people and to improve everyone's experience with services and digital products. Being able to talk with a wide range of people, learn about their needs, and use this to implement improvements across products and services is why I chose to become a desinger.
